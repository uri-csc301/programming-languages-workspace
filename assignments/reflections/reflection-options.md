# Reflection Options

You may choose any format that allows you to clearly demonstrate your understanding.

Below are some examples. You may also propose your own idea.

---

## Creative Formats

### Artistic Representation
Create a visual or artistic representation of the concepts.

Include:
- images or video of your work
- an explanation of the concepts and what your piece represents

---

### Journal Entry / Letter
Write a reflective journal entry or letter explaining the concepts.

---

### Podcast
Record an audio or video podcast explaining the concepts.

---

### Short Story
Write a story that incorporates the concepts.

---

### Video (Vlog / Mini-Lecture)
Create a video explaining concepts in your own way.

---

### Script (Movie/Show)
Write a script that demonstrates concepts through characters or dialogue.

---

## Application-Based Options

### Lesson Plan
Design a lesson or assignment that teaches the concepts.

---

### Interview
Interview someone about the concepts and reflect on it.

---

### Real-World Analysis
Analyze how a real system or technology relates to the concepts.

---

### Tutorial
Create a tutorial teaching the concepts.

---

## 🎯 Important Notes

- You may choose **any format**
- You may combine formats if you'd like
- Your work must still be:
  - accurate
  - clear
  - thoughtful
